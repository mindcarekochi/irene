Meet Dr. Irene

Dr. Irene Mary Ike, MBBS, MD (Psychiatry)

Dr. Irene Mary Ike is a highly skilled, empathetic, and licensed psychiatrist with over 4 years of clinical and academic expertise. She currently serves as a Senior Resident in the Department of Psychiatry at Believers Church Medical College Hospital, Thiruvalla. She secured Rank 4 in KUHS during her MD at GMC Thiruvananthapuram.

Beyond the clinic, Dr. Irene is a passionate mental health influencer and active community advocate. She frequently speaks at awareness programs in schools, tribal communities, and government institutes. Her personal mission is to erase mental health stigma while providing deeply personalized, non-judgmental healing.
